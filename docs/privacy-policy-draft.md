## The short version

- You can use ImpactMe **without an account**. Then everything stays in your browser on your device and nothing is sent to us.
- If you **create an account**, we store your data so it can sync between your devices.
- Your **journal, mood and energy entries** can say something about your health. We only store them in your account if you **agree to it**, and you can change your mind at any time.
- We **don't sell** your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.
- You can **download** your data and **delete** your account at any time, in the app.

## What we collect

**Without an account:** nothing reaches us. Your tasks, projects, journal entries and settings are stored only in your browser on your device.

**With an account:**

| What | Examples |
|---|---|
| Account details | Your email address and the name you give. Your password is stored only as a secure one-way hash, never in a form we can read. |
| If you sign in with Google | What Google shares with us: your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID. |
| Your app data | Tasks (with their notes, dates, times, priority, tags, subtasks, repeats and reminders), projects, and the settings that sync (name, time format, week start, calendar view, task sorting and reminder defaults). |
| Journal entries, mood and energy (**only with your consent**) | The text, title, mood, energy, tags and "grateful for" note of each entry. |
| Your choices | Your answers when you created your account: that you are 16 or older, that you agree to these terms, and whether you agreed to journal sync, each with the time you gave it. |
| Sign-in security records | When you sign in, and the IP address and browser used, kept by our sign-in provider to protect your account. |

We don't collect your phone number or location, and we don't build a profile of you.

## Your journal and health information

Mood, energy and journal entries can reveal information about your physical or mental health, which the law treats as special (sensitive) personal data. That's why we ask you separately before storing them in your account. If you say no, your journal stays on your device only, and everything else still syncs.

You can turn journal sync on or off at any time in **Settings › Privacy**. Turning it off deletes your journal entries from your account straight away; they stay on your device.

## Where your data is stored

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **See your data and get a copy**: Settings › Privacy › Download a Copy of My Data gives you everything on your device as a file you can keep or move elsewhere.
- **Correct it**: edit it in the app.
- **Delete it**: delete individual items, or delete your whole account in Settings › Account › Delete Account.
- **Withdraw consent**: turn journal sync off in Settings › Privacy. This doesn't affect anything done before.
- **Object** to how we use your data, or ask us to **restrict** it.
- **Complain** to Ghana's Data Protection Commission (**[DPC WEBSITE AND CONTACT]**), or, if you are in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

For anything you can't do in the app, email **[CONTACT EMAIL]**. We reply within **[30]** days.

## Age

ImpactMe accounts are for people **16 or older**. We ask you to confirm this when you create an account. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we'll delete it.

## Cookies and similar technology

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The app uses your browser's storage only for what it needs to work: keeping you signed in, your data and settings, and remembering your choices. These are strictly necessary, so no cookie banner is needed **[REVIEW]**.

## Security

Each account can only ever reach its own data. We test that two accounts can't see each other's data. Other safeguards include a security check against bots on sign-up and sign-in, sign-in links that only work once, asking you to confirm it's you before deleting your account, and keeping secret keys only on our servers. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data, we'll tell you in the app and, where the law requires, ask for your consent again.
